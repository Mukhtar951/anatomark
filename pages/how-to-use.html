HOW TO USE

Getting Started.

1

Print Your Marker

Download and print the marker on plain paper, at least 8 cm wide. A4 is ideal.

Get the marker →
2

Open a Structure

Browse the library and select any anatomy structure.

Open the library →
3

Launch AR View

Tap “Launch AR View” and point your camera at the marker. Then drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, and double-tap to reset.

TIP · GOOD LIGHT

Use bright, even light and avoid glare on the paper.

TIP · KEEP IT FLAT

Lay the marker flat with the whole black border in view.

TIP · DISTANCE

Hold your phone about 20–40 cm from the marker.

Before you start

  • A phone with a working camera and an internet connection (Wi-Fi is best, 3D models are several MB).
  • The latest Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). If you opened the link from WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook, open it in Chrome or Safari instead — those in-app browsers often block the camera.
  • Allow camera access when your browser asks. AnatoMark only uses the camera on the AR page and never records or stores anything.

Troubleshooting

The camera says “permission denied” or never turns on

Tap the lock or settings icon beside the web address, choose Permissions → Camera → Allow, then reload the page.

“Could not start video source”

Another app or browser tab is using the camera. Close video calls, camera apps and other tabs that used the camera, then reload. If it persists, restart your browser.

The camera works but the 3D model doesn’t appear

Keep the whole marker, including its thick black border, inside the frame. Hold it flat, about 20–40 cm away, in even light with no glare. A large model can take a few seconds to download first.

The model flickers or jumps around

The marker is partly hidden, creased, or reflecting light. Print it on plain (not glossy) paper and keep it flat on a table.

I don’t have a printer

Open the marker page on a second screen (laptop or tablet), turn the brightness up, and point your phone at it. You can also have it printed at a nearby business centre or print shop.

The model is too big or too small

Move your phone a little closer or further away, or pinch to zoom. Double-tap the model (or press Reset) to return to the default size.

Rotate and zoom don’t respond

Touch the model itself with one finger to rotate it, or two fingers to zoom. If the page scrolls instead, reload the AR view. Rotating works only while the marker is in view.
