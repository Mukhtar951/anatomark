    Privacy

    • The AR view uses your camera to see the marker. The video stays on your phone and is never uploaded.
    • AnatoMark has no accounts and does not ask for personal details.
    • The Capture button saves a picture to your own device only.
    • If you contact the project by WhatsApp or email, that message goes to the project owner through those services.

    Using AnatoMark

    • Non-commercial. This is an educational project and is not for sale or commercial use.
    • Educational use only. The models and descriptions are study aids, not medical advice, and not a substitute for textbooks, dissection or your lecturers.
